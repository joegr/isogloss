Caveats

    The accent field

    Everything drawn here is geometry the database constructed: Thiessen cells over the reference sites, isoglosses as the shared boundary between cells above and below a threshold, bundles where many isoglosses run together, and dialect regions clustered in feature space then unioned in map space.

    Diffusion of a sound change

    One simulator, four regimes. Watch where the wavefront appears: under wave it expands as a ring from the origin; under hierarchical it materialises around distant cities first and only later joins up. That difference is the whole argument of the design note.

    What this is doing

    Audio is framed at 25 ms / 10 ms, and every frame gets an energy, a zero-crossing rate, a pitch estimate from normalised autocorrelation, four LPC formants, and a set of spectral moments. Vocal-tract length is normalised out before anything is compared, because otherwise the system measures the speaker's height rather than their hometown.

    Phones are recognised by scoring each frame against acoustic templates and decoding with a Viterbi pass that carries duration and phonotactic costs. The language is then identified from the phone string plus rhythm metrics — PRLM, in Zissman's sense — and the audio is decoded a second time under the winning language's phonotactics.

    Fourteen sociophonetic measurements are extracted, each with a reliability. Those are compared against a field of reference varieties using a Gaussian process whose covariance is the heat kernel of a gravity/barrier interaction graph rather than a distance kernel: barriers, settlement hierarchy and migration links are inherited by the interpolator instead of bolted onto it.

    The answer is a posterior over the earth's surface, returned as credible-region polygons. It is usually broad, and often multimodal across continents. That is the honest information content of fourteen acoustic numbers, and it is why the output type is a geometry and not a coordinate pair.

    Known limits

    • The reference field is expert-approximated from the descriptive literature, not measured from a corpus.
    • The template recogniser is inspectable but much weaker than a trained model; the recogniser interface exists so one can be dropped in.
    • Second-language accent is a different axis from dialect geography. The pipeline flags it rather than silently placing a Spanish-accented English speaker in Spain.
    • Narrowband audio destroys F3, and with it rhoticity — the single most informative English feature.
